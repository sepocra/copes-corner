---
title: The Wrap Up
description: Someone gives the direction
pubDate: 2026-10-15
---

The game has a bubble. A moment where tension escalates. Words exchanged, fouls everywhere, plenty of opinions and perspectives.

The referee has a chance to restore order. Assess the situation and make a decision. Provide a direction that moves the game forward. A defined role that helps everyone understand the state of play.

Part communication. Part clarity. Part composure. Part perspective.

This plays out in some meetings too. Plenty of opinions. No noticeable consensus. The group silence echoes.

There are times when someone steps up to give the direction. The wrap up. It doesn't have to be the host.

Everyone knows when it's done well. Everyone silently knows when it was missing.
