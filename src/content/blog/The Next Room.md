Seeing kids experience different environments unlocks a growing world of imagination and possibility. New friends, daycare and even hospital visits.

That concept applies to workplaces too.

There's that Jim Rohn phrase. You are the average of the five people you spend the most time with. 

It plays out in teams, rooms and experiences. 

Which teams have you been part of? 
Which rooms have you been in? 
What experiences have you had in recent times?

In stagnant environments imagination and creativity are limited. That walk that unblocks a stuck thought. That shower that leads to the next idea. Small wins.

The biggest growth comes from new teams, new rooms and new environments. One addition changes the average. 

Awareness lets you choose how it works for you.
