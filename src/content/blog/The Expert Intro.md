---
title: The expert intro
description: Credibility starts somewhere
pubDate: 2026-11-03
---
A powerful opinion is shared. Everyone goes along with it.  The discussion continues. The exec, the specialist, the president, the special guest in a meeting.

In conversations where everyone is known and the hierarchy is clear, the opinion lands.

A room with new faces is different. The opinion doesn't land the same. The side conversation happens after. Who was that? Where do they fit?

The expert intro to the group builds trust when things are new. The expertise is unable to introduce itself.

Same opinion. Different acceptance.
