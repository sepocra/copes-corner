---
title: The Second Time
description: The first gets recognised, the next is overlooked
pubDate: 2026-11-09
---
That debut game at a new level. All the prep for a walk into the unknown. The celebration and sense of achievement at the end.

The second game slows down. Familiar faces. Reactions feel more natural. No one draws attention to the better performance.

Work follows the same pattern. The first time with a new business process. The notes are read. The questions get asked. The system seems familiar although the screens are different.

The second time the flow starts to appear. The steps are clearer. They make sense. The process is followed with no checking for guidance. 

The first got the attention. The second was a better result.
