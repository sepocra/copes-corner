The smile can become contagious. That one small moment impacts what happens next. 

Happiness spirals up amongst a group. It offsets a negative environment.

 Within the tensions of a game, a smile at the right time de-escalates a potential conflict. It impacts the whole game. 

Kids find something funny within a serious moment or laugh out loud to something mundane. This flows on to everyone within earshot. The smiles appear across the room.
